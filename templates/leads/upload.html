📋 CSV Format Guide for New Users

How to Prepare Your Leads File

Follow this simple layout so your emails personalize properly.

Accepted Column Headers:
email REQUIRED
name Optional (or first_name + last_name)
company Optional (or organization)
phone Optional (mobile number)
source Optional (portal / event / batch)
Option A: Full Standard Format (Best)
email,name,company,phone officer@gem.gov.in,Rajesh Sharma,GeM Commerce,+919876543210 buyer@railnet.gov.in,Anita Verma,Northern Railway,+919812345678 director@aiims.edu,Dr. K. Patel,AIIMS New Delhi,+919922334455 procurement@bhel.in,Suresh Kumar,BHEL Hyderabad,
Option B: Minimal Format (Emails Only)
email officer1@gem.gov.in buyer@railnet.gov.in procurement@bhel.in
💡 Key Rules to Remember:
  • In Excel: Save your workbook as CSV (Comma delimited) (*.csv).
  • Auto-Cleaning: Addresses like test@test.com or @mailinator.com are rejected automatically.
  • No Duplicates: Leads already in your database or previous bounces are skipped safely.
  • Scheduler Connection: Once stored, the scheduler pulls from these leads according to your daily cap.
